# Storefront Appearance

Set store-wide defaults for how offer widgets look — badge shape, button shape/style, and default colors. These act as the fallback whenever an individual offer doesn't override its own colors in the Offer Builder's design step, so you can keep every offer visually consistent with your brand without configuring colors one offer at a time.

Per-offer color overrides (set inside the Offer Builder) always take priority over these defaults.
