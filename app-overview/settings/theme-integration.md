# Theme Integration (App Embed)

UpsellSuite renders storefront offers through a theme app embed rather than direct Liquid edits. This section of Settings shows whether the embed is currently enabled on your live theme, and lets you jump straight to the Theme Editor if it isn't.

- **App embed is enabled** — offers are able to render on your storefront (individual offers still need to be published to actually appear).
- **App embed is disabled** — no UpsellSuite offers will render anywhere on your storefront, even if you have published offers. Click through to the Theme Editor and toggle it on.

{% hint style="info" %}
Embed status is per-theme. If you duplicate or switch themes, re-check this section after publishing the new theme.
{% endhint %}
