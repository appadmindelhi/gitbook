# Recommendation Widgets

Separate from the Offer Builder's wizard-based offers, UpsellSuite also provides standalone recommendation widgets — lightweight product grids you can drop onto key pages without building a full offer.

### Widget types

- **Related products** — shown based on the current product
- **Recently viewed** — the shopper's own browsing history on your store
- **Trending** — your best-performing products store-wide
- **New arrivals** — your most recently added products

### Placements

Product page, Collection page, or Home page.

### Configuration

Each widget has its own heading, product count, and grid/list layout, configured independently from the Offer Builder's offers — so you can run recommendation widgets and wizard-built offers side by side without conflict.
