# Product Add-on

Add-on offers let shoppers quickly add small, related items (like accessories or warranties) without leaving the product or cart page.

### Where it appears

Product page and Cart page.

### Layout

Renders as a horizontal scroll strip of add-on products with quick add-to-cart controls.

### Discounts

Percentage, fixed-amount, or fixed-price per add-on.

### Cart awareness

Add-ons already in the cart are hidden from the widget and reappear automatically if removed.
