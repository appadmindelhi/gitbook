# Frequently Bought Together (FBT)

FBT offers pair a product page's main product with one or more companion products, shown as a bundle the shopper can add to cart together.

### Where it appears

Product page only.

### Layout

Displays as a stacked or horizontal bundle row alongside the main product, showing each item's price and a combined "Add bundle to cart" action.

### Discounts

FBT bundles support percentage, fixed-amount, or fixed-price discounts on the combined bundle.

### Cart awareness

If a companion product is already in the shopper's cart, it's automatically excluded from the bundle so you never offer something they've already added (`exclInCart`).

### Countdown timers

Optional session-based or fixed-end-date countdown to add urgency.
