# Cross-sell

Cross-sell is UpsellSuite's most flexible offer type — it's the only type available on every placement: Product page, Cart page, Post-purchase, and the Thank-you page.

### Layouts

- **Product page** — Carousel or Grid (labeled "Stacked" in the picker; renders as a vertical list).
- **Cart page** — Carousel only.
- **Post-purchase / Thank-you** — single-offer or bundle presentation depending on placement.

### Discounts

Percentage, fixed-amount, or fixed-price, applied per product or as a bundle depending on layout.

### Targeting

Cross-sell supports the full set of [targeting rules](../targeting.md) — cart contents, cart value, item count, customer tag/type/country, past orders, and discount code presence.

### Countdown timers

Available on Product page, Cart page, and Cart Drawer placements.
