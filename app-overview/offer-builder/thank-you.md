# Thank-you Page Offers

Thank-you page offers appear on the order status page after checkout completes.

### How it works

Because a checkout extension on the order status page cannot modify the order that was just placed, accepting a Thank-you page offer opens a **new cart** with the offered product added, rather than appending to the existing order. This is a Shopify platform constraint, not a limitation specific to UpsellSuite.

### Offer type

Cross-sell only.

### Triggering

Offers can be shown based on the contents of the order just placed — useful for suggesting a natural next purchase (e.g. suggesting refills, accessories, or a bundle discount code) right after checkout.

### Bundle mode

Thank-you offers support a "bundle" layout paired with a discount code, letting you present a multi-product bundle with its own discount rather than a single product add.
