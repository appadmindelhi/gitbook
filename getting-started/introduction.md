# Introduction

UpsellSuite helps Shopify merchants increase average order value (AOV) and revenue per customer by surfacing relevant offers at the right moment in the shopping journey — without requiring any code changes to your theme.

### What UpsellSuite does

UpsellSuite lets you build and manage offers across five surfaces:

| Surface | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Product page** | FBT, Cross-sell, or Add-on offers shown alongside a product |
| **Cart page / Cart drawer** | Cross-sell or Add-on offers shown while the customer reviews their cart |
| **Post-purchase** | A one-click upsell shown immediately after checkout, before the order confirmation page |
| **Thank-you / order status page** | Cross-sell offers shown after the order is placed, opening a new cart |

Alongside offers, UpsellSuite also includes standalone **recommendation widgets** (Related products, Recently viewed, Trending, New arrivals) that can be placed on product, collection, or home pages independently of any specific offer.

### How offers reach your storefront

Every offer you build is automatically synced to your theme and kept up to date — no manual embed or liquid editing required beyond the one-time app embed activation covered in the [Installation Guide](installation.md).

### Revenue attribution

UpsellSuite tracks impressions, accepts, and completed-order revenue for every offer, so your Analytics always reflect real, attributed performance rather than estimates.
